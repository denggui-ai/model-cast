How to use: put your clothes on the model Yuning

This method is not fully verified yet: it has been tested four times on ChatGPT Pro (once each for a side view, back view, looking back, and sitting sideways). Whether all four results retained the same person is still awaiting the shop owner’s confirmation. Other ChatGPT plans have not been tested.

[Three steps to one image]
1. Open the ChatGPT website and start a new chat.
2. Upload two things:
   - the 4 photos in the "2_Upload_to_AI" folder;
   - your own product photo (a front photo works best).
3. In the same message box, write one sentence saying what you want, and send it together with the photos. You do not need to copy any prompt. For example:
   - "Put my garment on this model, full body, front view"
   - "Change only the top; keep the trousers and shoes"
   - "Make a half-body image"
   - "Make a back view" - upload a photo of the back of the product as well; without one, do not make back views.
   If your product photo shows several items (for example a top and shorts), say clearly in that sentence which items to change, especially if you only want to change one item. If you do not, the AI may change only one of them.

[Check 4 things in the result]
1. Is it still the same person? Compare with Model_card.png in the "1_Read_me (do not upload)" folder and look at the two memory points first: full rounded cheeks with a small rounded chin; softly arched brows with almond eyes, outer corners slightly downturned.
2. Was her body changed? Longer legs or a slimmer waist?
3. Is the garment right? Compare with your product photo: colour, neckline, sleeves, buttons or zip, length.
4. Was anything else changed? Background, pose, shoes, extra jewellery or bags.

[If something is wrong]
- Small problem (one detail of the garment, extra jewellery): say what is wrong in the same chat, e.g. "The neckline should be round; keep everything else."
- Her face no longer looks like her: do not keep editing that image. Start a new chat and upload those 4 photos and the product photo again.
- You cannot recognise her in a full-body image: ask for a half-body image first.
- Next product: start a new chat for each product, from those 4 photos. Do not continue from the previous result.
- If the same problem is still there after two fixes, stop and start again in a new chat.

[Folders]
- 1_Read_me (do not upload): this guide, the model card, originality and limits. For you only; do not upload them to the AI.
- 2_Upload_to_AI: upload these 4 photos.
- 3_Lifestyle: walking, sitting and smiling images you can use in your shop as they are. Do not use them as a face reference.
