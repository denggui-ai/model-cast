# Limits of use (please read before use)

- No guarantee that her face never changes; no pixel-level preservation.
- The model pack says nothing about your product: what the garment looks like is defined by your product photos.
- Limited testing does not mean every garment or angle will work. No promise of global uniqueness or higher sales.
- With only a front product photo, side and back results are not accurate product images. Without a back photo, do not make back views.
- For side and back results where the face is not clearly visible, "is it the same person" is **unverified**.
- In full-body images the face is small and can drift to a generic face. If you cannot recognise her in a full-body result, ask for a half-body image first.
- This pack covers this one model only; a second model is unverified.
